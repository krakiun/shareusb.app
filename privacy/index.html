ShareUSB — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 19 October 2026

About the app

ShareUSB (bundle id com.krakiun.shareusb) is a desktop app for Windows, macOS and Linux that lets a technician work on Android phones and USB devices connected to another computer, after the owner of that computer approves the request. It is published by krakiun-lab (krakiun.com). The app's server runs at usb.krakiun.com.

How it works

Each copy of the app receives a permanent 9-digit ID. A technician connects to that ID, and the owner of the computer accepts or rejects the request and chooses what to allow (phones, USB devices, or both). After that, the data between the phone or USB device and the technician's computer passes through our server, encrypted with TLS. Our server only forwards this traffic; it does not store it, so screen images, files and commands exchanged with a phone are never saved on our side.

Files you download from a phone are saved only on your computer, and files you upload go only to the phone you chose.

What we collect

Device identity: the 9-digit ID and a secret generated by our server. We keep only a hash of the secret.

Optional account: if you create an account, your e-mail address and password. The password is stored hashed (bcrypt), never in clear text.

Connection data: the IP address of the computers that connect to our server. It appears in the server logs, and the IP address of a technician is shown to the person who has to approve them.

Sharing and usage records: if you share a device with other users, we store what is shared, with whom, and until when. We also keep a usage log (which device was used, by which ID, when and for how long) so the owner can see who used their devices. Blocked IDs, groups and group credits are stored too.

AI connections (optional): if you connect an AI assistant to your account, we store the access tokens for that connection and the list of tools you allowed. You can revoke them in the app, and they are revoked automatically when you change your password.

Crash reports

When the app crashes, it sends a crash report (app version, operating system, technical error details) to Sentry, a crash-reporting service. Reports do not include your e-mail, passwords, files or phone contents.

E-mails

We send transactional e-mail only: account confirmation, password reset codes and links, and security notices about your account. Delivery is handled by Amazon SES. No newsletters, no marketing.

What we do not do

No advertising, no analytics or tracking SDKs, no sale of data, and no sharing with third parties beyond the providers needed to run the service: our hosting provider, Cloudflare (network protection in front of our server), Amazon SES (e-mail) and Sentry (crash reports).

Security

Traffic between the app and our server is encrypted with TLS. Nobody can connect to your devices without your approval in the app, and you can disconnect or block a technician at any time. Passwords and device secrets are stored hashed.

Retention and deletion

Device identities, accounts, sharing records and usage logs are kept while you use the service. To delete your account and the data linked to your ID, send a request through https://krakiun.com/contact/; we remove it from active systems promptly and from backups within a reasonable period, normally no more than 30 days. Server logs are kept only as long as needed to run and protect the service.

Your rights

You have the right to access, rectify, erase, port and object to the processing of your personal data. For any request, write to us through https://krakiun.com/contact/.

Children

The app is a professional tool and is not directed at anyone under 16.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date.

Platform

A complete platform for sharing repair tools.

Everything a network of shops needs to share dongles and phones, with order and accountability.

Remote USB & ADB

Raw USB dongles and phones over ADB, tunneled securely. They appear locally to the technician; their tools work directly on them.

Access control

A 9-digit ID and approval per connection. Separate permissions for phones and USB. You decide who, what and for how long.

◱

Groups with credits

Organize colleagues into groups with a credit economy: 1 credit = 1 minute. Whoever uses pays, whoever owns earns.

↻

Flash-safe reconnection

A network drop mid-flash doesn’t compromise the session: it resumes automatically, with the device still attached.

⚙

Always-on service

Runs as a Windows service (SYSTEM), starts at boot and keeps shares active with no session open.

▤

Queue & audit

FIFO queue with position and ETA, per-turn limits, and a full usage log: who, what, when and how long.

Security & control

Access always stays under your control.

Traffic is encrypted in transit, and every connection goes through the owner’s approval. Every turn is logged and any technician can be blocked instantly.

  • Approval per connectionNo one accesses a device without the owner’s explicit consent.
  • Full auditA log of usage and actions — including operations via AI (MCP).
  • Port-level isolationA port allowlist that prevents unauthorized access (anti-SSRF protection).

How it works

From sharing to working, in three steps.

Step 01

Share

Connect the dongle or phone and make it available to a colleague via their ID, or to the whole group.

Step 02

Approve

The technician requests access with your ID. You get a request and approve it — once or permanently.

Step 03

Work

The device appears locally to the technician. When done it detaches automatically, and the turn settles in credits.
